Our Story

About Wamambo

The Founder

Charles Cheuka Wamambo

With over 30 years of experience in the African tourism industry, Charles Cheuka Wamambo has dedicated his life to sharing the authentic beauty, culture, and heritage of Zimbabwe and beyond with the world.

His deep knowledge of indigenous cultures, wildlife ecosystems, and sustainable tourism practices has made Wamambo Tourism Afrika a trusted name among discerning travelers seeking meaningful African experiences.

Under his leadership, the company has grown from a small local operator to a respected pan-African tourism brand, while maintaining its commitment to community empowerment and environmental stewardship.

Our Mission

To provide transformative travel experiences that celebrate Africa's cultural heritage, wildlife, and natural beauty while empowering local communities and preserving the environment for future generations.

Our Vision

To be Africa's most trusted and celebrated luxury tourism brand - recognized for authentic storytelling, sustainable practices, and experiences that inspire a deep connection with the continent's soul.

Journey

Our History

1996

Charles Cheuka Wamambo founds the company, pioneering cultural tourism in Zimbabwe.

2005

Expansion into regional safaris covering Botswana, Zambia, and Mozambique.

2012

Recognized for excellence in sustainable tourism by the Zimbabwe Tourism Authority.

2021

Rebranded as Wamambo Tourism Afrika (Pvt) Limited, reflecting a pan-African vision.
